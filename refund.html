Refund Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-29

The short version

Because the report is delivered instantly, we do not refund purchases made by mistake or changes of mind. If something is broken on our side, we fix it or refund it.

What we do refund

  • You paid but never received the file.
  • The file is corrupted or will not open, and we cannot fix it.
  • You were charged twice for one order.

What we do not refund

  • You did not like your result.
  • You expected a different type of product.
  • You bought it twice by accident and both copies were delivered.

How to ask

Email support@auraquestlab.com within 7 days of purchase with your order number. We reply within 2 business days.

Statutory rights

Nothing here limits rights you have under mandatory consumer law in your country.
