Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-29

Who we are

AuraQuest Lab publishes self-reflection quizzes. We are the data controller for the information described below.

What we collect

  • Quiz answers. Scored in your browser. We do not store your individual answers on our servers.
  • Result type. An anonymous label such as "The Reacher", used to improve our quizzes. Not linked to your identity.
  • Email address. Only if you choose to give it, for order delivery and product updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Payment details. Handled entirely by Gumroad, our merchant of record. We never see or store your card number.

Who processes your data

Payments and order delivery: Gumroad (merchant of record), including applicable VAT and sales tax collection. Analytics: Microsoft Clarity and Vercel Analytics. Email: ImprovMX (forwarding only).

Cookies and analytics

We load analytics only after you accept the cookie banner. Vercel Analytics is cookieless. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and to object to processing. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Retention

Email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe, or 24 months after your last interaction. Order records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Children

Our products are intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
